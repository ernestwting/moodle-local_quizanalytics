# References

1. C. Sangwin, T. Hunt, et al., "STACK Assessment System,"
   [https://stack-assessment.org/](https://stack-assessment.org/).
2. T. Takada, S. Higuchi, and Y. Nakamura, "Visualization of Solution
   Processes to Reach the Correct Answer in Online Math Tests," in
   *Proceedings of the 18th International Conference on Educational Data
   Mining*, pp. 635–639, July 2025.
3. M. Neugebauer, S. Falk, R. Erlebach, S. Higuchi, and Y. Nakamura,
   "Robust & Reliable Automated Feedback Using Tree Edit Distance for
   Solving Open Response Mathematical Questions," in *Proceedings of the
   17th International Conference on Computer Supported Education*, vol. 2,
   pp. 618–626, April 2025.
4. A. Kurihara and Y. Nakamura, "Network Analysis of Solution Processes in
   Math Online Tests," in *Companion Proceedings of the 15th International
   Learning Analytics and Knowledge Conference (LAK'25)*, pp. 257–259,
   March 2025.
5. S. Higuchi and Y. Nakamura, "Classification of Answers in Math Online
   Tests by Visualizing Graph Similarity," in *Companion Proceedings of
   the 14th International Learning Analytics and Knowledge Conference
   (LAK'24)*, pp. 197–199, March 2024.

Citations [2]–[5] are the basis for [Solution Process
Visualization](calculations.md#solution-process-visualization) and its
tree-edit-distance charts.
